# Yoga to Transform Journey

Shared language for the client-led Journey and its coaching support.

## Language

**Client**:
A person using the Journey with support from a coach.
_Avoid_: user

**Challenge**:
An optional activity a client chooses to explore for a day. A challenge day counts when the client tries at least two of the three chosen activities.
_Avoid_: task, required habit

**Playbook**:
A client-owned collection of patterns, learnings and session insights the client chooses to keep in their own words.
_Avoid_: to-do list, coach notes

**Pause & Choose**:
Supportive options a client can use when pausing around a food-related urge.
_Avoid_: Menu

**Private Journey link**:
A link that grants its holder permission to view and update that client’s Journey.
_Avoid_: read-only share link

**Progress**:
A record of chosen challenges and Playbook learnings, without missed-day or failure states.
_Avoid_: streak, score

**Meal ideas and shopping list**:
Recipe discovery, meal planning and shopping lists are outside the current app’s scope.
_Avoid_: meal tracking
